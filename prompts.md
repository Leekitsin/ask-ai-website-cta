# Ask AI Prompt

The concept uses a pre-filled prompt to introduce a business and then allow the visitor to start a conversation with an AI.

## Prompt

Tell me about this company as a technology partner for businesses.

Start by giving me a clear, concise overview of what the company does.

Explain that the company helps businesses with technology solutions across areas such as hardware, software, cloud and productivity platforms, business technology, technology procurement, software solutions, deployment, implementation, professional services, managed services, device and IT lifecycle management, and ongoing support.

Present the company as a technology partner that helps businesses choose, deploy, manage and support the right technology for their requirements, rather than as a single-brand reseller.

After giving this overview, ask me only one question:

"What are you interested in?"

Do not ask me about my industry, company size, technology environment, budget, or other qualification questions unless I choose to provide them.

Once I answer what I am interested in, help me understand the most relevant solutions and services offered by the company.

Do not invent products, services, partnerships, capabilities, or customer examples that have not been provided in the company's information.
